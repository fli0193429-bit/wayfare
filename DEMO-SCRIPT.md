# Recording plan — about 2 minutes

Record only after a real AI draft and a buyer-approved sandbox capture have both passed. Use the sample brief below; keep credentials and personal details out of the recording.

0:00–0:15 — Show the planner page.
“Wayfare helps independent travel planners turn a customer request into a clear service quote and a deposit checkout.”

0:15–0:40 — Paste and submit this sample brief:
“Two adults, four nights in Genoa in late May. We want a quiet hotel, maritime museums, and a day trip by train. Hotel budget €150 per night. We can arrive on either Friday or Saturday.”
“AI drafts the scope, exclusions and questions. It does not choose the price or claim that anything is booked.”

0:40–1:05 — Show the actual returned questions. Review and edit the scope. Set example planning fees of €240 total and €60 deposit.
“The planner reviews the draft and sets the fee. This is a planning service, not a hotel reservation.”

1:05–1:40 — Open the customer link. Show the agreed scope, exclusions and €60 deposit. Approve checkout with a sandbox buyer account.
“The customer reviews the quote and approves payment in PayPal Sandbox. This demonstration uses test money.”

1:40–2:00 — Show the actual successful receipt only after capture succeeds.
“The server checks PayPal’s completed capture and the expected amount before displaying this receipt.”

End with the repository URL and setup instructions. Do not invent a successful receipt or replace a failed AI call with a prewritten result without labeling it.
